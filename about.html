Nice to meet you!

Hello! I'm Alejandra, a multidisciplinary designer specializing in branding, UI/UX and illustration, based in Vancouver, BC. Born and raised in Lima, Peru, my creative journey has always been shaped by curiosity and storytelling. After studying Design Formation at Langara College, I transferred to Capilano University to focus on strategic, narrative-driven design. I love creating meaningful work that blends creativity, clarity, and human connection. Outside of design, I enjoy films, novels, travel, and painting portraits of my dog.
