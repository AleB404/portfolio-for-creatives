Everyone Everywhere

Multiplayer Cultural Game

Everyone Everywhere brings people together by letting them experience life from different cultural perspectives. Through fun mini-games inspired by real-world challenges, children ages 7–10 learn how diverse traditions, customs, and social situations shape everyday life, nurturing empathy and curiosity through play.

Designed by Alejandra Bernuy and Tanner McMahon

  • Digital Product
  • UI/UX
  • Game Design
  • Art Direction
  • Illustration

Change of direction

The project started as an app helping immigrants in Canada navigate local social conventions. But research made us question its premise: it asked newcomers to adapt without inviting others to do the same. That insight shifted our vision. We reimagined it as a shared experience that promotes understanding and celebrates cultural diversity across the world.

Challenge

  • Limited understanding of experiences beyond one’s own.
  • Language and cultural barriers create distance.
  • Existing tools focus on assimilation, not exchange.
  • Few inclusive spaces for cultural interaction.

Our Approach

  • Players explore diverse cultural perspectives through play.
  • Non-verbal, collaborative gameplay builds connection.
  • Creates a playful, inclusive environment.

Visual Identity

Moodboard

We chose bright colours to evoke enthusiasm and reflect the game’s diversity.

Illustration style

Initially aiming for semi-realistic characters, we ultimately opted for simplified, stylized, and memorable designs that support personalization and fun, immersive animations.

Celebration

Short mini-game rounds inspired by each country’s culture, traditions, and everyday life turn global differences into moments of fun and discovery.

Education

Players learn by doing. Each interactive mini-game invites them to tap, move, and make choices as they explore different cultural settings. With information kits available during and after gameplay, every challenge becomes a chance for hands-on learning and deeper cultural understanding.

Collaboration

Players work together to complete tasks, learning from each other and depending on one another to succeed. Collaboration is emphasized through team animations and interactive friend interactions, making every contribution meaningful.
